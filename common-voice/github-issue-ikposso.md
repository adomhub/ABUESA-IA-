# Welcome to the Common Voice Community!

Thank you for your interest in adding a new language to the Common Voice Spontaneous Speech platform and dataset.

## Necessary metadata

- Language to add to Spontaneous Speech: Ikposso (also written Akposso / Ikpɔssɔ), spoken in the Wawa and Amou prefectures of Togo and in Ghana
- ISO code: kpo (ISO 639-3)

### About this request

This request is made on behalf of WABUƐ (https://adomhub.github.io/ABUESA-IA-/), a community project for the Ikposso language and Akposso culture, by Alexi (native speaker). The questions were written in Ikposso by a native speaker and reviewed by other speakers from our community. None of them asks for personal information.
