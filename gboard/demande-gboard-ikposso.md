Please add the Ikposso language (also called Kposo / Akposso, ISO 639-3: kpo) to Gboard.

Ikposso is spoken by the Akposso people in the Plateaux Region of Togo (Wawa and Amou prefectures) and in eastern Ghana. It uses the Latin alphabet with these extra letters: ɛ ɔ ɩ ʋ ɣ ɖ ŋ ǝ, plus tone accents (á à â).
